FAQ

Who is eaZSt for?
eaZSt is built for content creators, streamers, and digital entrepreneurs who generate passive income. If you earn money through YouTube, Twitch, Patreon, Shopify, or automated bank deposits and want the ultimate "dopamine hit" of seeing what you earned while you slept, this app is for you.
What are your thoughts on internet privacy?
We believe your data belongs strictly to you. In an era where user data is the product, we take the opposite approach: strict data minimization. We do not sell your data, we do not broker it to ad-networks, and we do not use your financial history to train global AI models.
Is my financial data secure?
Yes. All third-party connections are explicitly "Read-Only". We cannot move your money. We use Plaid for bank-level security, and all connections are tokenized. Furthermore, your personalized AI insights are generated locally on your phone's hardware, ensuring your financial metrics never touch a third-party LLM cloud server.
So how do you encrypt data?
We utilize a secure hybrid architecture. The app uses anonymous authentication, meaning we never see or store your passwords. Your active session data is locked locally inside your device's hardware-backed Keystore. The necessary OAuth tokens used for background syncs are stored on our secure backend inside an encrypted PostgreSQL database.
Why is eaZSt only for Android?
Apple's rigid iOS ecosystem and restrictive developer guidelines block the exact background processes and system-level event receivers required to build our core "sleep tracking" features. Android respects developers and provides the necessary freedom to build powerful, unrestricted background automation.
