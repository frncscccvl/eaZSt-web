App Purpose & Integration

eaZSt is an analytics dashboard built for creators. By securely connecting to platforms like YouTube and Google AdSense, the app aggregates your overnight ad revenue, channel memberships, and video metrics. Our localized, on-device AI engine then synthesizes this data into a morning financial report—helping you track your passive income momentum with complete privacy.
