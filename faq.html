Frequently Asked Questions

Why does eaZSt need access to my YouTube / Google AdSense accounts?

eaZSt is a passive income dashboard. We request read-only access to your YouTube and AdSense metrics so the app can securely aggregate your overnight video revenue, channel memberships, and page views. This allows you to wake up to a personalized morning report showing exactly what your portfolio did while you were sleeping.

Do you sell my data?

Absolutely not. Your financial data is securely retrieved for your own personal visualization. We do not sell, broker, or share your data with advertising networks, third-party data brokers, or external AI trainers. For full details, see our Privacy Policy.

Is my data safe with the AI feature?

Yes. eaZSt uses Gemma, a localized, on-device AI. Your financial metrics and questions are processed directly on your phone's physical hardware (edge computing). Your data never leaves your device to hit cloud LLM APIs.

How do I delete my data?

You own your data. You can sever connections individually inside the app's Connections Hub. For a complete account wipe, open the app, go to Settings -> About, and tap the App Version 4 times. This permanently deletes your local tokens, anonymous database records, and server webhook maps instantly.
